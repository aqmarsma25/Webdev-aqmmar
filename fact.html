5 FACTS ABOUT MANCHESTER UNITED

Most English League Titles

Manchester United has won a record 20 English top-flight league titles, 13 of which were secured under manager Sir Alex Ferguson.

Historic 1999 Treble

In 1999, United became the first English club to win the Premier League, FA Cup, and UEFA Champions League in a single season.

Resilience After Munich

After losing eight players in the tragic 1958 Munich plane crash, manager Sir Matt Busby rebuilt the team to win the European Cup ten years later.

Unbroken Academy Streak

Manchester United has included at least one homegrown youth academy graduate in every matchday squad for over 4,200 consecutive games since October 1937.

Railroad Origins

Founded in 1878 as Newton Heath LYR F.C. by local railway workers, the club was renamed Manchester United in 1902 after a group of investors saved it from bankruptcy.
